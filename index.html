Why Opera Up Close?
Most people experience opera from the back of a theater. We invite you to experience it from across the dinner table.

In a beautifully curated vintage Chicago apartment, Opera Up Close brings together professional vocal artistry and inspired cuisine for an evening that is equal parts concert, dinner party, and community building.

From the first course to the final aria, every detail is crafted with intention. Alex Hinand, culinary creator and lifestyle visionary with a devoted following, designs a menu that matches the drama and elegance of the evening. John Concepción, a professional tenor with decades on the operatic stage, performs live just feet away from you, creating a unique artistic experience.

There is no distance between you and the art. There is only the music, the food, and the feeling that you are exactly where you are supposed to be.

The evening

How the night unfolds

Every evening follows the shape of an opera, act by act. The music and the menu change with each month's theme.

  1. OvertureArrival

    Guests arrive and greet each other. Specialty non-alcoholic drinks and appetizers are served.

  2. Entr'acteWelcome

    A few words from the hosts about the evening and what to expect.

  3. Act IThe music

    Arias and songs on the evening's theme, sung by tenor John Concepción and accompanied by pianist Paul Hamilton.

  4. Act IIDinner

    Everyone moves to the dining room for dinner and conversation.

  5. Act IIIDessert

    Guests return to the living room for coffee and dessert while John and Paul perform more arias and songs on the evening's theme.

  6. EncoreTo take home

    Each guest leaves with a goodie bag of hand-crafted cookies, made by Alex.

Good to know

Before you come

Do I need to know anything about opera?

Not at all. Plenty of guests have never been to an opera house. You'll hear a little about each piece before it's sung, and you're welcome to ask questions over dinner.

What should I wear?

Whatever makes you feel like you're out for a nice evening. Some guests dress for the theme, some come straight from work. Both are welcome.

Can you work with dietary needs?

Email us about allergies or restrictions once you have your ticket, and Alex will plan around them.

Where is it?

In John and Alex's apartment in the Ravenswood neighborhood of Chicago. The address and arrival details come with your ticket.

How do tickets work?

Tickets are sold through Eventbrite, usually about two weeks before each evening. Each one is announced to our mailing list first, and seats tend to sell out within hours of that email. Buying a ticket is the only way to hold a seat, so joining the list is the best way to get one.
